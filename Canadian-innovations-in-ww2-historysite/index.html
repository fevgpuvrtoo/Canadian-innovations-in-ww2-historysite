The Jasper Experiment — Field Intelligence Summary

  • Spring 1943: Built in total secrecy by conscientious objectors and NRC engineers in Jasper National Park, Alberta — under NRC President C.J. Mackenzie's direct supervision.
  • Weight: 1,000 tons. Refrigerated through the Alberta summer using a single 1-horsepower compressor pumping chilled air through galvanized steel ducts inside the Pykrete hull.
  • Strategic purpose: To prove the concept for a full 2,000-foot, 2,000,000-ton aircraft carrier that could operate from mid-ocean, beyond the range of Axis land-based aircraft and beyond the reach of torpedoes.
  • Outcome (1943): The prototype survived the summer intact. However, by late 1943 the introduction of long-range B-24 Liberator aircraft and mass-produced escort carriers closed the Atlantic Gap — ending the strategic need for Habakkuk before full construction began.
  • Today: The sunken wooden framing and refrigeration pipes remain at the bottom of Patricia Lake — an underwater monument to Canadian wartime science, still visited by scuba divers.

CHRONOLOGICAL MASTER LOG: 1939–1945

The Rapid Escalation of Canadian Innovation

Follow the chronological march of discoveries that reshaped military doctrine and modern peacetime life.

1939
COMMUNICATIONS

Donald Hings Patents the Battlefield Packset

Canadian engineer Donald L. Hings re-engineers his mining field radio for the Canadian military at the NRC in Ottawa, creating the early CMX-1 and future C-58 Walkie-Talkie.

1940
PHYSIOLOGY & FLIGHT

Dr. Wilbur Franks Tests the Anti-G Suit

At the Banting and Best Institute at the University of Toronto, Dr. Franks constructs Canada’s first human centrifuge and rides it himself while wearing the water-filled Franks Flying Suit.

1941
INTELLIGENCE

Camp X & "Hydra" Established on Lake Ontario

Sir William Stephenson opens the ultra-secret paramilitary espionage camp near Whitby/Oshawa with the massive high-speed "Hydra" transmitter linking London, Ottawa, and Washington.

1942
SYNTHETIC POLYMERS

Polymer Corporation Founded in Sarnia

In response to the Fall of Singapore and the loss of natural rubber, Canada establishes Polymer Corp to convert oil refinery gases into synthetic rubber, supplying the entire Allied war machine.

1943
SECRET EXPERIMENTS

Project Habakkuk Secret Testing on Patricia Lake

Under orders from Winston Churchill and the Combined Chiefs of Staff, a 1,000-ton model of an unsinkable Pykrete aircraft carrier is built and refrigerated in Jasper National Park, Alberta.

1944
BIOTECHNOLOGY

Connaught Laboratories Mass-Produces Penicillin for D-Day

Deep-tank fermentation techniques perfected in Toronto deliver millions of units of life-saving penicillin to Normandy beachheads, virtually eradicating gangrene among Allied wounded.

1945
NUCLEAR ENERGY

ZEEP Reactor Achieves Criticality at Chalk River

Canada’s Zero Energy Experimental Pile (ZEEP) becomes the first operational nuclear reactor outside the United States, launching Canada's peaceful CANDU reactor and medical isotope industry.
