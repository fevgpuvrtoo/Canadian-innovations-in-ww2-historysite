# Northern Ingenuity: Canadian Innovations in World War II

An interactive historical web application presenting Canadian scientific, biomedical, and engineering breakthroughs in World War II. Features hands-on interactive simulators and verified citations from **Encyclopaedia Britannica**, **The Canadian Encyclopedia**, **The Canadian War Museum**, and the **National Research Council of Canada**.

## 🎮 Hands-On Interactive Simulators

1. **Dr. Wilbur Franks' G-Suit Centrifuge & Flight Simulator (`#gsuit-sim`)**
   - **Interactive Controls:** G-Force throttle slider (1.0 G to 8.5 G) and Franks Liquid-Filled Flying Suit toggle switch.
   - **Visual HUD:** Real-time cockpit viewport with horizon roll.
     - *Without Suit:* Blood pools in lower extremities, causing progressive tunnel vision at 4.5 G and full G-LOC blackout at 5.5 G+.
     - *With Franks Suit:* Incompressible liquid bladders automatically apply hydrostatic counter-pressure, keeping vision clear at sustained combat turns up to 8.5 G!
   - **Physiological Telemetry:** Real-time readouts for Cerebral Blood Pressure (mmHg), Heart Rate (BPM), and Water Bladder Pressure (PSI).
   - **Verified Sources:** [Britannica: G-suit](https://www.britannica.com/technology/g-suit) • [The Canadian Encyclopedia: Wilbur Franks](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/wilbur-rounding-franks)

2. **Pykrete Material Multi-Stress Lab 2.0 (`#lab-simulator`)**
   - **Interactive Controls:** 3 stress test modes (Ballistic .303 Rifle Round, Blowtorch Thermal Flame, 5,000-lb Hydraulic Crush) + Wood Pulp percentage slider (0% to 20%).
   - **Live Reactions:**
     - *0% Pulp (Pure Ice):* Catastrophically shatters under bullets, rapidly liquefies under torch flame, and shears under pressure.
     - *14% Pulp (Canadian Pykrete):* Interlocking cellulose fibers absorb bullets within 2 inches, form an insulating crust under torch heat, and sustain 5,000 lbs like reinforced concrete.
   - **Verified Sources:** [The Canadian Encyclopedia: Project Habakkuk & C.J. Mackenzie](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/chalmers-jack-mackenzie) • [Canadian War Museum](https://www.warmuseum.ca)

3. **Battle of the Atlantic: NRC Type 268 Radar & U-Boat Hunter (`#radar-hunter`)**
   - **Interactive Controls:** Live 360-degree rotating phosphor cathode-ray radar sweep canvas, Pulse Microwave Radar button, Deploy Canadian CAT Acoustic Decoy button, and Drop McGill RDX Depth Charges button.
   - **Combat Simulation:** Hunt surfaced German U-boats in thick North Atlantic fog, deploy towed noisemakers to divert acoustic homing torpedoes, and destroy targets with high-impulse RDX shockwaves.
   - **Verified Sources:** [Britannica: Radar & PPI](https://www.britannica.com/technology/radar) • [The Canadian Encyclopedia: Battle of the Atlantic](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-the-atlantic) • [Britannica: RDX](https://www.britannica.com/science/RDX)

4. **Donald Hings' C-58 Walkie-Talkie Field Comms Station (`#radio-station`)**
   - **Interactive Controls:** Vintage frequency tuner dial (4.0 to 6.5 MHz), Patented Donald Hings Voice Noise Filter toggle switch, and Push-To-Transmit (PTT) button.
   - **Audio-Visual Feedback:** Intercept the Allied 3rd Division transmission at 5.80 MHz. Without the filter, hear heavy artillery blast interference; engage Hings' noise filter to isolate clear voice speech.
   - **Verified Sources:** [Britannica: Walkie-talkie](https://www.britannica.com/technology/walkie-talkie) • [The Canadian Encyclopedia: Donald Lewes Hings](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/donald-lewes-hings) • [D.L. Hings Foundation](https://dlhings.ca)

5. **Wartime Science Intelligence Exam (`#quiz`)**
   - 5-question evaluation with immediate historical feedback and declassified clearance rank calculation.
